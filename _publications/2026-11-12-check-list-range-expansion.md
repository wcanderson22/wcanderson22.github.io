## Description

Geographic distributions of many bird species have shifted in recent decades. Here, we present evidence that two desert southwest species of passerine bird, the Crissal Thrasher, *Toxostoma crissale* Henry, 1858, and the Cactus Wren, *Campylorhynchus brunneicapillus* (Lafresnaye, 1835), are expanding their breeding ranges northward into novel geographic areas in the state of Colorado.
