## Research Overview

My research investigates the evolutionary processes
that generate and maintain biodiversity, with a
particular focus on avian evolutionary genomics,
speciation, and chromosome evolution.

I integrate whole-genome sequencing, population
genomics, comparative genomics, and field ecology
to understand how genomic variation contributes
to evolutionary divergence.

## Current Research

### Sex Chromosome Evolution in Birds

My research examines the evolution of sex chromosomes,
including chromosomal fusions, inversions, and
the emergence of nonrecombining genomic regions.

### Genomic Divergence and Hybridization

I investigate how populations diverge despite
ongoing gene flow, with particular emphasis on
hybrid zones and genomic regions associated with
reproductive isolation.

### Avian Phylogeography and Speciation

I use genomic and phenotypic data to investigate
evolutionary relationships, population structure,
and species boundaries in birds.
